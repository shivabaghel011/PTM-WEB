About Us & PTM-WEB

Meet the developers and researchers behind the Post-Translational Modification Interaction Webserver.

Our Mission

At the NCR Biotech Science Cluster, our goal is to build computational biology platforms that accelerate proteomics research. PTM-WEB is designed as a centralized gateway to explore writer and eraser enzyme kinetics, target substrates, and topological network connections from functional datasets.

Research Team

Our laboratory focuses on structural bioinformatics, protein network modeling, and computational mass spectrometry data parsing. This database is part of our ongoing effort to catalog biochemical signaling complexes.

Reference: If you use PTM-WEB in your publication, please cite: Yadav et al. PTM-WEB: A Curated Webserver for Protein Modification Networks (2026).
