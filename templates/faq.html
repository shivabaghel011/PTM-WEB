Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find answers to common questions about PTM-WEB datasets, filtering, and visualization.

Q1: What are Writers and Erasers in PTM-WEB?

Writers are enzymes that catalyze the addition of post-translational modifications (e.g. acetyltransferases, methyltransferases). Erasers are enzymes that remove these modifications (e.g. deacetylases, demethylases).

Q2: How is the STRING threshold score used?

The threshold filters out low-confidence protein-protein interactions. Scoring ranges from 0.0 to 1.0 (with 0.400 representing medium confidence and 0.700 representing high confidence).

Q3: Where can I find the filtered species?

All available species in the dropdown pass the UniProt proteome quality filters: CPD classification as either Standard or Close to standard, and BUSCO completeness $\ge 90\%$, fragmented $\le 5\%$, and missing $\le 5\%$.

Q4: How do I export the interaction networks?

On the results page, you can use the viewport buttons in the top-right of the network canvas: the expand icon fits the network to the screen, and the download arrow exports a PNG file directly.
